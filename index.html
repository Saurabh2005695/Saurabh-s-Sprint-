CONTROLS & RULES

Mobile Touch Controls

  • SWIPE LEFT / RIGHT: Switch between the 3 railway tracks
  • SWIPE UP: Jump over roadblocks, hurdles & low barriers
  • SWIPE DOWN: Slide under high girders & fast-fall mid-air
  • DOUBLE TAP: Activate Hoverboard shield (Crash protection)

Desktop Keyboard Controls

  • Arrow Left / A: Move Left
  • Arrow Right / D: Move Right
  • Arrow Up / W: Jump
  • Arrow Down / S: Slide & Duck
  • Space / B / E / H: Activate Hoverboard Shield
  • P / Esc: Pause & Resume Game

Power-Ups

  • Coin Magnet: Attracts all coins into your bank
  • 2X Multiplier: Doubles your score rate
  • Hoverboard: Protects from 1 fatal crash
  • Jetpack: Soar high above obstacles with free sky coins
